Quick start

  1. Switch on and wait for the GPS light to go green.
  2. Hold the Ride button 1 s to start. The lights flash green.
  3. Ride.
  4. Hold the Ride button 4 s (until all red) to finish. Connect the phone and the ride copies over.

Buttons

Ride button: hold 1 s = start. While riding: hold and let go on blue = pause; hold until red (4 s) = end. Paused? Just tap Ride to carry on.

Marker button: tap to mark a spot (crash, a line to try). A press that can't do anything flicks red.

Lights

BatteryGreen over 50% · yellow 20-50% · red under 20% · flashing red under 10%. When it's flat the ride is saved and the unit switches itself off - charge it, then press Ride.
GPSGreen strong · orange weak · red none yet.
RecordingBlinking white = recording · fast = waiting for GPS · slow glow = paused (tap Ride to carry on) · amber = storage getting low · fast red = can't record.
PhoneBlinks blue while connected.

Tracks

Ride 3 laps somewhere new and the track sets itself up. Change the lines from the Tracks tab. Red laps (a stop, or a possible cut) don't count - tap Count it if one was fine.

Timer

Pick a time (or, at a saved track, a number of laps) on the Live tab before you ride. When it's up all the lights flash white until you press either button (that press does nothing else). The ride keeps recording - stop when you're ready.

Start practice

At a track, tap the Ride button 3 times quickly (or Practise a start on the Live tab). Sit still - all the lights go red, then green after a few seconds. Go! Your reaction, 0-50 km/h and time to the start distance (set per track in the Tracks tab, 60 m to begin with) show in the ride's Insights. Several starts in a row with no laps make a Start practice ride. Moving on red is a jump start - the lights flash red and it's saved as a jump. Do another straight away: stop, sit still, tap 3 times.

Insights

Open a track ride and tap Insights: time left to find, where you lose time turn by turn (tap one to see it on the map), lap by lap and your turns - name them, add or delete them. Notes go in the box under the ride's name.

It looks after you

Over the line: all the lights flash the lap colour - purple best, green faster than last lap, yellow slower.

Forgot to start? Riding without recording, the lights flash amber. Hold Ride 1 s to start, or tap a button to ignore it.

Track or trail: as soon as you do a full lap of a saved track, the ride becomes a track session by itself - riding there from home included.

Something not working?

Can't connect: Android - use Chrome. iPhone - use the Bluefy app. Still stuck: switch the unit off and on.

No GPS: it needs open sky, not a shed. Give it a minute.

No lap times: check the ride is on Track, not Adventure.

Progress
Sessions

After 3 laps somewhere new, a finish line goes on the fastest straight by itself, as long as no other part of the track comes within reach of it. Wider catches every pass even with GPS wobble; narrower fits tight tracks.

Riding mode

Track times laps. Adventure records the ride and its stats. New rides use this; you can also switch any ride afterwards.

Live timing

Sends your laps and position to a base station for live timing. Leave it off when you're not racing - it uses a little more battery.

The name the unit shows in Bluetooth. Leave it empty to go back to its MX-… name.

All rides
Backup

One file with every ride, your tracks, names, notes and lap edits. Keep it in Google Drive. Import puts back anything this phone doesn't already have - on a new phone, or after clearing the browser.

Firmware update

After it restarts, leave it switched on for 30 seconds. If the new version doesn't start properly, the unit goes back to the old one.
