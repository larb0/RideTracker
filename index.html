Quick start

  1. Switch on and wait for the GPS light to go green.
  2. Hold the Ride button 1 s to start. The lights flash green.
  3. Ride.
  4. Hold the Ride button 4 s (until all red) to finish. Connect the phone and the ride copies over.

Buttons

Ride button: hold 1 s = start. While riding, a short hold (let go on blue) = pause / resume; hold until red (4 s) = end.

Marker button: tap to mark a spot (crash, a line to try). A press that can't do anything flicks red.

Lights

GPSGreen strong · orange weak · red none. Amber blinks = a warning (connect the phone to see it).
RecordingBlinking white = recording · fast = waiting for GPS · slow glow = paused.
PhoneBlinks blue while connected.

Tracks

Ride 3 laps somewhere new and the track sets itself up. Change the lines from the Tracks tab. Red laps (a stop, or a possible cut) don't count - tap Count it if one was fine.

Something not working?

Can't connect: Android - use Chrome. iPhone - use the Bluefy app. Still stuck: switch the unit off and on.

No GPS: it needs open sky, not a shed. Give it a minute.

No lap times: check the ride is on Track, not Adventure.

Sessions

After 3 laps somewhere new, a finish line goes on the fastest straight by itself, as long as no other part of the track comes within reach of it. Wider catches every pass even with GPS wobble; narrower fits tight tracks.

Riding mode

Track times laps. Adventure records the ride and its stats. New rides use this; you can also switch any ride afterwards.
